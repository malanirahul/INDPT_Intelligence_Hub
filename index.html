Illustrative data. Every number on this page is synthetic, generated in the browser from a seeded model of a multi-brand F&B group with 24 stores in five cities. It is not Independent Food Company data. Store names are placeholders. It shows how the hub would work once POS, reservations, delivery platforms, app, labour, budget and survey data are connected.

Definitions. Net revenue = gross sales after discounts, in AED (KSA converted). Contribution = net revenue − food cost − variable labour and packaging − platform commission − direct delivery and payment cost (before rent and fixed overheads). Labour/Sales = scheduled labour cost ÷ net revenue. Known customer = identified through reservation, app, wallet or Wi-Fi sign-in. NPS = % promoters − % detractors; target 55. Dayparts: breakfast 7–11, lunch 11–15, afternoon 15–18, dinner 18–22, late night 22–03.

Methods. Forecast = seasonal model (same weeks last year × recent year-on-year momentum) with a band of ±1.28σ of backtest error. Target = budget where one exists; beyond the budget year, last year × the growth the budget was running at in the last 13 weeks. Campaign value = (treatment conversion − control conversion) × audience × AOV × (1 + repeat rate) × contribution margin. Action impacts are estimated annual contribution; none is counted as value until a holdout group proves it. YTD compares the same weeks this year and last year. 2026 actuals run to the week ending 27 Sep 2026; the 2026 budget covers the full year. Analytics models (propensity, churn, CLV, recommendations, forecasting, insight generation) sit underneath the opportunities as enablers.
